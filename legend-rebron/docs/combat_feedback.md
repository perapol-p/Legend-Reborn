# Critical hits and combo
Critical damage is rolled once when an attack is created using the player's Crit chance and Crit damage stats. Melee and hitscan attacks notify immediately on a confirmed hit. Projectiles, piercing waves, delayed tornado volleys and explosions carry their own critical flag, so changing weapons or rolling another hit does not change an in-flight attack. The HUD shows CRITICAL! and damage below the upper-right combo for 1.1 active seconds. Misses, blocked shots and ignored damage do not notify.
Combo hits grant 1 point; kills grant an additional 3. Each rank covers 20 points: F 1-20, E 21-40, D 41-60, C 61-80, B 81-100, A 101-120, S 121-140, SS 141-160 and SSS 161+. Points have no gameplay cap and keep growing beyond SSS. After 3 active seconds without a scoring hit, 20 points are removed every 3 seconds. Excess points at SSS decay gradually rather than being truncated to a rank threshold. Pausing freezes decay and the notification.
